Frequently asked questions.

Answers about registration, accounts and the demonstration portal.

How do I create an account?

Select Register, choose Personal or Corporate, complete the requested details and submit your request.

Why is my account pending?

New accounts require administrator approval. You will be able to access the customer dashboard after approval.

Can I transfer real money?

No. Transfers in this version are simulated internal test transactions. No real money moves.

How can I get a demonstration balance?

An authorised platform administrator can assign a test balance from the admin dashboard.

Can I deposit or withdraw from a bank or crypto wallet?

No external deposit or withdrawal service is connected in this demonstration.

How do I contact the team?

Use the Contact page. The current form displays an on-screen acknowledgement and is not yet connected to email delivery.

Is this a licensed financial institution?

This demonstration website does not claim to be a licensed bank, payment institution or financial service provider. Do not rely on it to hold or move funds.
